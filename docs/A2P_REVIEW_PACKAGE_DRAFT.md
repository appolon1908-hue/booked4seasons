## Message flow

Customers opt in by visiting `https://booked4seasons.com/request-service`, entering their mobile number, and voluntarily checking an unchecked box authorizing Booked4Seasons service-request and appointment text messages. The disclosure states that message frequency varies, message and data rates may apply, consent is not a condition of purchase, and users may reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help. Terms are available at `https://booked4seasons.com/sms-terms` and the Privacy Policy is available at `https://booked4seasons.com/privacy`. Consent applies only to the Booked4Seasons messaging program and is not transferred to independent service providers.
